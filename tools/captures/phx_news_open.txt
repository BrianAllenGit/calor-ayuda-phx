Dedicated Heat Relief Sites Close For Fall, Winter
Dedicated Heat Relief Sites Close For Fall, Winter

October 01, 2026

Heat Response and Mitigation

Share:




UPDATE (October 1, 2026) The planned closure of dedicated heat relief centers listed below took effect on October 1 at 9 a.m. Heat relief is always available during normal business hours at Phoenix public libraries, where air conditioned space is open to all, regardless of the date.

For information on heat relief offerings, data, weather trends, and findings from the 2026 heat season, check out the weekly heat reports.

Dedicated Heat Relief Locations (Now Closed For the Season)

24/7 Respite Center: 20 W. Jackson St.

Justa Center: 1001 W. Jefferson St.

Cholla Library: 10050 Metro Pkwy E. (still available for heat relief during regular business hours)

If you or someone you know needs help, call 2-1-1.

For more information on the services Phoenix offers each summer, visit Phoenix.gov/summer or the browse the Office of Heat Response and Mitigation's webpages.
