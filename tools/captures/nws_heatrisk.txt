NWS HeatRisk is supplementary to official NWS heat watches, warnings, and advisories. It provides guidance for decision makers and those who are sensitive to heat . For more information, view the tabs above the map.
Category	Risk of Heat-Related Impacts
Green
0	Little to no risk from expected heat.
Yellow
1	Minor - Primarily affects those who are extremely sensitive to heat and without cooling/hydration.
Orange
2	Moderate - Affects those who are sensitive to heat, especially those without cooling/hydration, and some health systems and industries.
Red
3	Major - Affects anyone without cooling/hydration as well as health systems and industries.
Magenta
4	Extreme - Rare and/or long-duration extreme heat with no overnight relief affecting anyone without cooling/hydration as well as health systems, industries, and infrastructure.
